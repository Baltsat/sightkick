## New: what the fixes broke

**A. The gap fix (item 5) pushed the manifest flap directly on top of the hi-hat pad.** In the prior capture the `Choose a song to begin` box sat low, below the hi-hat's position, fully clear of the kit. Closing the "dead gap" by switching to `flex-start` + a tighter `gap` pulled the box upward, and it now visually sits on top of the hi-hat cymbal — only a sliver of the pad's rim shows below the box edge (confirmed by crop). The team knew this: `KitHome.css:52-57` and `:60-71` explicitly document "text sits over live strike zones (hi-hat/crash reach ~14-35% from the left edge)" and add `pointer-events: none` on the wrapping box so a click still reaches the pad underneath. Functionally the pad still works. Visually it does not — a solid dark text panel now sits on the one thing on this screen that's supposed to read as "unmistakable at two metres." Fix: either move the shelf flap to not overlap any hotspot's bounding box, or reduce its footprint/opacity so the pad glyph reads through it, the way the studio wash already does for the rest of the photo.

**B. The crash cymbal is still hidden behind the hero title (pre-existing, not new, but worth naming precisely now that it's the worst offender left).** The green ring sits directly behind the word "Warm-Up" — confirmed present in both the prior and current captures, so this didn't regress, but it also didn't get touched, and now that kick/snare/floor-tom are legible, the crash and hi-hat are the two zones dragging the screen back below "unmistakable at two metres." Both trace back to the same root cause: the title/manifest column and the kit photo are not laid out with any awareness of where the eight hotspots actually sit. That's the real fix needed for item 17 and finding A/B together — not more copy tuning, but giving the manifest column a text-safe reserved area that the kit-zone map treats as off-limits, per the spec's own instruction to "reserve a stable text-safe edge; leave cymbals, heads, and strike zones physically visible."

## Screen-by-screen: place or report?

- **Home** — closer, but not there. The kit bleeds full-bleed and three of eight zones now genuinely glow like an instrument. But the title still reads as a caption laid over a photograph rather than the identity of the room, and two zones (crash, hi-hat) are now covered by text/UI rather than merely faint. A player who sat down here would find the kick, snare, and floor tom inviting; he'd have to guess at the crash and hi-hat until he zoomed in mentally past the manifest.
- **Songs** — now a library, not an admin table. Row grammar matches the reference: art, title, one line, one honest status. This screen went from the worst in the set to the cleanest fix in the set.
- **Journey** — a real place now. One caption, real lock glyphs, a real controls button, and season names restored at the compact width (even if truncated). The drum-strewn room with a path winding between them reads as a place a rehearsal happens, not a curriculum spreadsheet.
- **Practice** — passes. Transport line is honest and complete at both widths, subtitle doesn't break mid-word, score stays legible. The bar highlight bands are taller now and read less like "a small object in an empty room," though true bare paper below the staff persists at roughly the same size as before — not urgent, as previously noted.
- **Result** — the biggest turnaround in the set. Truthful headline, two factual cells, evidence gated behind an explicit disclosure, visible continuation action, flat full-page paper instead of a card-on-a-card, and a difficulty tag that no longer contradicts the lesson it's attached to. This is the model the other screens should be measured against now.
