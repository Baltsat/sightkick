# kb.push pass — the kit-is-the-launcher rebuild, judged

Captures reviewed: `docs/design-qa/2026-08-13-push/*.png` (1225×768 and 1024×700, all six routes including the new Statistics/Insights capture) against `docs/design-acceptance-notes.md`, `docs/visual-system-v3.md`, `docs/kit-launcher-design.md`, the live Yandex captures in `docs/design-qa/2026-08-12-yandex-reference/`, and my own prior verdicts in the other `2026-08-13-*` folders. Home is not a variation on the surface I judged before — diffed against `2026-08-13-kb14-final/01-home-1225x768.png`, this is a full rebuild: `My Wave` is gone from the rail, every one of the eight strike zones now carries a two-line text label, and the old unlabelled-oval pattern is retired. I judge it fresh against `kit-launcher-design.md`, the doc that authored this decision, and I went past the screenshots into `HomeCockpit.tsx`, `SongView.tsx`, `NotationGlossary.tsx`, and `LibraryCandidateList.tsx` to confirm what the pixels imply. Songs, Journey, Practice, and Result are largely unchanged from the state my prior passes already scored; I do not re-litigate what's already fixed there except where new evidence changes the picture.

No praise below. Ranked list closes the document.

---

## Home — a labelled diagram of a kit, not yet the kit itself

**First read:** the kit fills the frame edge to edge, one huge title (`Alternating Singles Warm-Up` over a small `LESSON 01.01` kicker) sits across the top the way `My Vibe` owns its frame, and every strike zone now carries a name. That structural part is real — `My Wave` is gone from the sidebar, confirmed against `kit-launcher-design.md`'s explicit instruction ("это не отдельный My Wave должен быть"). But three things are broken badly enough that a player sitting down would not read this as a room he can just start hitting.

1. **The kick pad and the bottom-left button are the same control, twice, both labelled `Continue`.** This is not a visual-similarity call — I read the source. `HomeCockpit.tsx:913` wires the kick zone's click handler to `executeDoor('kick')`; `HomeCockpit.tsx:906-916` wires the standalone bottom-left button to the identical `onClick={() => executeDoor('kick')}`, and both fire on a single mouse click with no confirm delay (`handlePointerStrike` at `HomeCockpit.tsx:707-720` calls `executeDoor` immediately; the confirm-then-fire two-step at `HomeCockpit.tsx:735-742` only gates real MIDI input events, not pointer clicks). Two controls, one label, one destination, both live in the first viewport at once. `kit-launcher-design.md`'s entire premise is "Home has no menu of features. It has a drum kit, and every drum is a door" — this button is exactly the menu-of-features item the redesign exists to delete, and it's sitting directly beside its own duplicate door. `visual-system-v3.md` says the same thing from the other direction: "Remove supporting buttons that route to the same result." Confirmed at both captured widths (`01-home-1225x768.png`, `01-home-1024x700.png`).

2. **Three of eight doors currently open onto the identical destination, with identical label text, and nothing on screen explains why.** `HomeCockpit.tsx:522-546` sources the kick's `Continue` detail from `continuationSong?.name`, the snare's `My Wave` detail from `myWaveSession?.launch.candidate.title`, and the hi-hat's `Next lesson` detail from `nextLesson?.candidate.title` — three independently-computed values that, on this account, all resolve to the same string. Cropped directly: kick reads `Continue — Lesson 01.01 — Alternating Singles …`, snare reads `My Wave — Alternating Singles Warm-Up`, hi-hat reads `Next lesson — Alternating Singles Warm-Up`. The whole point of "every drum is a door" is that a door tells him what's behind it before he hits it; right now three doors tell him three different things are behind them (resume my last spot / my personal stream's pick / the curriculum's next lesson) and then show the exact same song with no acknowledgment that they collapsed onto one target. A brand-new account — the exact user this screen is built for — is the account most likely to hit this collision on day one, not an edge case he'll never see.

3. **The labels read as UI chips stuck onto a photograph, not light on a drum.** Zoomed crop of the kick pad: a solid, near-opaque dark rounded rectangle sits on the drumhead with white bold `Continue` and a lighter subtitle line inside it, with a hard-edged colour ring around the whole oval. This is the visual grammar of a tooltip or a museum-tour hotspot, not paint or light on an instrument. `design-acceptance-notes.md` names the bar directly: strike zones must be "unmistakable... while still looking like light on a drum rather than stickers." The prior faded-ring problem is gone, but the fix went straight past "light on a drum" to "sticker," which is the other half of the same sentence the acceptance notes reject. Compare to `My Vibe`: one glowing image field and one huge word, nothing that reads as an annotation layer. This home screen is now the busiest-looking screen in the whole set — eight opaque label cards competing for attention at once — which is the opposite of the one-dominant-object law this whole rebuild is supposed to serve.

4. **The kicker `LESSON 01.01` is clipped at 1024×700**, not just tight on margin — cropped at the very top pixel row, the tops of the letters are cut by the window edge. Confirmed by direct crop of `01-home-1024x700.png`, rows 0–50px: only the lower half of each glyph is visible. This is the same "zero top margin" issue named in the `2026-08-13-final` critique, now advanced from "no room to breathe" to literal clipping at the width he's likely to actually run this at.

5. **The kick pad's own title still truncates with an ellipsis** (`Lesson 01.01 — Alternating Singles …`, both widths) even though it breaks on a word boundary. Given the label itself is the newly-added feature this round, showing a truncated version of the one thing (`Alternating Singles Warm-Up`) that the hero title above already displays in full is a strange choice — the pad either needs a shorter string (it doesn't need the `Lesson 01.01 —` prefix, the hero already carries that) or a wider text-safe box.

6. **Three of eight doors are permanently dead ends on this account, dressed up as promises.** `Top 1 / Top 2 / Top 3` all read `Play to set this tom` — identical copy, no differentiation between why he'd choose one tom over another, and per the Songs screen (`0 in your library`) there is no possible content behind any of them yet. `kit-launcher-design.md`'s own honesty rule says an empty zone "offers the nearest real thing" — `Play to set this tom` doesn't say what playing it does, doesn't explain the mechanic, and is repeated three times. On the one account this screen is designed for — day one, nothing owned yet — three of the kit's eight doors are decorative.

## Songs (`02-library-*.png`) — the row grammar holds; the placeholder-art bug is still live, in a sibling file the earlier fix missed

Header (`0 in your library · 0 ready to play · 243 to add from your playlists`) agrees with every row (`Not in your library yet`) — that truthfulness fix from the `truth` pass holds. Row shell — thumbnail, title, one support line, one right-side status — matches the reference grammar.

But the fallback-art-is-the-app-logo defect flagged in `2026-08-13-final/critique.md` (finding 3) is **not fixed, it moved**. Cropped directly from `02-library-1225x768.png`: every visible row's thumbnail is a circular gold-ring badge with a cross/spoke mark — the Drumroll brand mark, not a neutral placeholder. I checked why: `SongListItem.tsx:10,223,226` and `SongListView.tsx:17,1327` were fixed — both now import a real, distinct `assets/song-art-placeholder.svg` (a cream rounded square with a music-note glyph, nothing like the logo). But the component actually rendering this screen's rows is neither of those — it's `LibraryCandidateList.tsx`, which still does `import appIcon from '../../../../assets/icon.png'` (line 10) and uses it as both the missing-art fallback (`song.albumCover ?? appIcon`, line 147, with an `onError` handler that re-forces it at line 150) and a second, unconditional usage at line 322. The fix landed in the two files that don't render this screen and was never applied to the one that does. Every one of the ~243 unowned rows still reads as self-branded merchandise.

## Journey (`03-journey-*.png`) — holds, no new defects found

Season rail, lock glyphs, one `NEXT UP` manifest, `Controls` as a real pill button. The stray ellipsis after `Foundations` in the season plaque named twice in prior passes is gone in this capture (cropped and confirmed clean at 1225×768) — not re-flagged.

## Practice (`04-practice-*.png`) — a permanent kit-navigation legend, confirmed in source, not a screenshot artefact

`SongView.tsx:2953` renders `<NotationKitKey layout={notationLayout} />` unconditionally, every render, no toggle, no dismiss, no collapse — I read `NotationGlossary.tsx:62-107` end to end and there is no state prop, no summon/dismiss wiring on this component at all (that machinery exists on the sibling `NotationGlossary`/`useNotationGlossaryIntent`, which this is not). `visual-system-v3.md`'s own removal list bans exactly this: "a permanent kit-navigation legend on a practice screen." At 1024×700 it occupies roughly the bottom fifth of the score canvas by itself, present in the idle `Ready` state before he's played a single note — this is "preloaded," not "reachable," which the spec also separately forbids ("Detail is reachable, never preloaded... None belongs in the default first viewport while the kit or score is asking for a hit").

Separately, and smaller: at 1024×700 the transport title collapses to `Lesson 01.01 –…` with zero words of the actual song name visible (cropped and confirmed) — this predates this round (identical in `2026-08-13-kb14-final/04-practice-1024x700.png`), so it's carried-forward, not new, but no prior critique named the title itself rather than the subtitle beneath it.

## Result (`05-result-*.png`) — holds

Truthful headline (`This pass did not connect` over `0 of 132 notes landed`), two factual cells, evidence gated behind disclosure, one ember continuation action visible without scrolling at both widths. Matches the model state from the `truth` pass. Nothing new to flag.

## Statistics / Insights (`06-statistics-*.png`) — new capture, holds structurally

Reads like a real evidence surface, not a dashboard deck. Nothing severe found; not a focus of this pass since it wasn't part of any prior rejection.

## Ranked, most severe first

1. **Home: the kick strike zone and the bottom-left `Continue` button are the same action, both visibly labelled `Continue`, in the same viewport** (`01-home-*.png`; `HomeCockpit.tsx:906-916` vs `:913` and `:707-720`) — the literal menu-of-features control this rebuild's own doctrine (`kit-launcher-design.md`) says must not exist, sitting next to its own duplicate door. Code-confirmed, not a rendering coincidence.
2. **Home: kick, snare, and hi-hat all resolve to the identical song and show identical text (`Alternating Singles Warm-Up`), with nothing on screen acknowledging the collision** (`01-home-*.png`; `HomeCockpit.tsx:522-546`) — three doors that claim three different kinds of "next," one of them a personal stream that's supposed to weigh joy against readiness, collapse into one indistinguishable answer on exactly the account — day one, nothing else in range — most likely to hit this.
3. **Songs: every unowned row's thumbnail is still the app's own brand mark, not a neutral placeholder** (`02-library-*.png`; `LibraryCandidateList.tsx:10,147,150,322`) — the fix for this exact defect landed in `SongListItem.tsx` and `SongListView.tsx` but was never applied to `LibraryCandidateList.tsx`, the component that actually renders this screen. ~243 rows read as self-published, not as a personal library with missing art.
4. **Practice: a permanent kit-navigation legend renders unconditionally on every state, including idle** (`04-practice-*.png`; `SongView.tsx:2953`, `NotationGlossary.tsx:62-107` — no dismiss/toggle exists on this component) — an explicit, named "must die" pattern from `visual-system-v3.md`, occupying roughly a fifth of the score canvas at 1024×700 before he's played a note.
5. **Home: strike-zone labels are opaque dark UI pills with hard borders, not light on a drum** (`01-home-*.png`) — the previous "faded to invisible" defect is gone, replaced by the other failure mode the same acceptance-notes sentence rejects; eight simultaneous label cards make this the visually busiest screen in the set, working against the one-dominant-object law the rebuild is meant to serve.
6. **Home: the `LESSON 01.01` kicker is clipped by the window's top edge at 1024×700**, not just tight on margin — the tops of the glyphs are cut (`01-home-1024x700.png`, rows 0–50px).
7. **Home: three of eight doors (`Top 1/2/3`) are permanently dead ends on a zero-song account**, all reading the identical, mechanic-free `Play to set this tom` (`01-home-*.png`) — decorative real estate on the one screen that's supposed to have no menu of features.
8. **Home: the kick pad's own label still truncates with an ellipsis** (`Lesson 01.01 — Alternating Singles …`, both widths) despite the hero title two lines above already showing the full name in full — redundant and incomplete at once.
9. **Practice: the transport title collapses to `Lesson 01.01 –…` at 1024×700 with none of the actual song name visible** (`04-practice-1024x700.png`) — pre-existing (identical in `2026-08-13-kb14-final`), not caused by this round, not previously named at the title level.

Journey, Result, and Statistics hold. Songs' row grammar and header-truthfulness hold — only the placeholder-art regression is open there, and it's a narrower, one-file fix now that the actual broken component is identified. Practice's transport/notation/caption pattern from prior passes is otherwise unchanged. Everything else wrong this round is concentrated on Home, which is the screen that changed the most and the one the product's entire premise depends on.
