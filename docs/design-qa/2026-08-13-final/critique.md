# kb.13 final pass + icon fix — does it hold, and did the icon actually ship?

Captures reviewed: `docs/design-qa/2026-08-13-final/*.png` (1225×768 and 1024×700) and `docs/design-qa/2026-08-13-icon/*` against `docs/design-acceptance-notes.md`, `docs/visual-system-v3.md`, `docs/design-qa/2026-08-12-yandex-reference/`, and my own prior verdicts in `2026-08-13-truth/critique.md`, `2026-08-13-room/critique.md`, and `2026-08-13-finish/critique.md`. I diffed `2026-08-13-final` byte-for-byte against `2026-08-13-kb13-final` (same filenames, both present): Home, Library, Journey, Practice, and Result all differ, but every diff traces to one cause — the new sidebar brand mark. The screen content, copy, and layout are otherwise pixel-identical to the already-judged kb13-final state, so the five open items from `2026-08-13-room` stand as previously scored (3 fixed, 2 partly) and I do not re-litigate them below except where new evidence changed the picture.

One provenance fact matters more than it looks: `2026-08-13-kb13-final` is a genuine installed-app capture — its `capture-installed.mjs` launches `/Applications/Drumroll.app/Contents/MacOS/Drumroll` by explicit path. `2026-08-13-final`'s `capture-final.mjs` does not launch the installed app at all; it launches `out/main/index.js` straight from the working tree (its own header comment: "reflects the current working tree including every lane's uncommitted edits"). The two sets are not the same kind of evidence, and that distinction is load-bearing for the icon finding below. I also went past the screenshots into the actual filesystem: the shipped `assets/icon.png`, the `assets/icon.iconset/` PNGs, and — critically — the real `.icns` inside the installed, notarized `/Applications/Drumroll.app` and the packaged `release/kb13-final-20260813T062753Z` DMG. That is not decoration; it changes the verdict.

**Headline: the new icon is a real, verified improvement at small size, and a real, verified regression in "beautiful" at full size, and — this is the load-bearing fact — it has not shipped to the app that is actually installed on this machine.**

## The icon, judged on the brief's own two-part test

### Does it read at 16px in a Dock full of other icons?

Yes, and this part is well-proven. I pulled the actual production files — not re-renders — from `docs/design-qa/2026-08-13-icon/winner-shipped/16.png`, `reference-sources/kb5-downscaled/kb5-16.png`, and `reference-sources/current-downscaled/current-16.png`, scaled all three nearest-neighbor (no smoothing that could flatter or hide anything) to the same size, and put them side by side:

- **kb.5** — a soft brown blob with a pink/orange horizontal smear. Warm, but no drum, no sticks, no legible shape; at 16px it's a color, not an icon.
- **current (rejected)** — almost exactly the owner's complaint: a near-black square with a barely-visible pale oval. This is the worst of the three by a wide margin.
- **winner (Signal Disc)** — a distinct dark ring on a warm gold field with a small orange peak inside. This is the only one of the three that reads as a deliberate shape rather than a smear, at true 16×16 pixels, unscaled.

So: yes, it is more recognizable than both of its predecessors at 16px. That claim in the icon README holds up under direct pixel inspection, not just the README's own comparison grid.

One caveat worth naming: the palette is muted — cream, gold, dark umber, one orange accent — next to the fully saturated jewel tones most Dock neighbors use (Messages green, Photos' rainbow, Safari blue). It is legible on its own; it will not be the most attention-grabbing icon in a busy Dock. Not a defect, just the honest answer to "is it recognizable" rather than "does it pop."

### Is it beautiful at full size, and better than both?

Only half-true, and the icon README overstates the other half. Compare `winner-shipped/1024.png` against `reference-sources/kb5-icon-1024.png` directly:

- **kb.5 at 1024px** is a photorealistic rendered snare drum — real chrome lugs, real head material, ambient occlusion, a glowing pink-to-gold waveform arcing across the head. It has genuine material richness and warmth.
- **The winner at 1024px** is a flat vector mark: a dark ring, eight lug-dots, one gradient waveform spike on a flat gold field. It is clean, on-brand, and competently executed — but it is a poster-simplified badge, not a rendered object. Next to kb.5 it reads as the _simplified_ icon, not the _upgraded_ one.

The icon README's verdict — "also being the boldest, most premium mark at 1024px... the only candidate that passes both halves of the brief's own test at once" — is not supported by putting the two files next to each other. The winner is the right trade for legibility (correctly prioritized, since the owner's actual complaint was about legibility, not beauty), but calling it more beautiful than kb.5 at full size is a claim the evidence in this same folder contradicts. The honest answer: **better than the current (black) icon at both sizes; better than kb.5 at 16px; worse than kb.5 at 1024px.** "Better than both, full stop" is not true.

### The one fact that overrides both of the above

I checked what is actually installed. `/Applications/Drumroll.app/Contents/Resources/icon.icns`, converted back to PNG and inspected, is the **old black snare with the small orange starburst** — the exact icon this whole exercise exists to replace. Byte-for-byte identical to `release/kb13-final-20260813T062753Z/mac-arm64/Drumroll.app/Contents/Resources/icon.icns`, the packaged, notarized DMG already sitting in `release/`. `defaults read` confirms this installed bundle is `1.2.13` (i.e. exactly the kb.13 build the task names as the reference install). Its `Info.plist`/binary/icon were all written at 14:32; `assets/icon.iconset/*` and `assets/icon.png` were regenerated at 15:37 — **over an hour after** this exact app was built and notarized. There is no newer packaged build anywhere in `release/` that contains the new icon.

So: every judgment above about "is it beautiful, does it read at 16px" is a judgment about SVG and PNG files sitting in `assets/` and `docs/design-qa/`. It is not yet a judgment about anything the owner will actually see. Right now, today, the Dock icon, the Finder icon, the Cmd+Tab switcher, the About panel, **and the in-app sidebar brand mark** all still show the icon he explicitly rejected ("раньше классная была" — the old one was good, this one isn't). I initially read the sidebar mark in this round's captures as proof the fix was at least partly live; it isn't. `docs/design-qa/2026-08-13-kb13-final/capture-installed.mjs` launches `/Applications/Drumroll.app/Contents/MacOS/Drumroll` by explicit path — that folder is a genuine installed-app capture, and its sidebar mark is the old dark icon. This round's `docs/design-qa/2026-08-13-final/capture-final.mjs` launches `out/main/index.js` straight from the working tree instead (its own header comment says so: "reflects the current working tree including every lane's uncommitted edits") — a dev/source-tree run, not the packaged app. `assets/icon.png` is bundled into the renderer at build time, so an app built at 14:32 cannot be showing an icon file written at 15:37 no matter where in the UI it's drawn; the new mark in today's Home/Library/etc. captures only exists because those captures never touched the installed binary. **The owner sees the new icon nowhere in the actual app right now — sidebar included.** This needs a fresh build → notarize → install cycle before the icon fix is real to him anywhere, not just real in the repo.

## New finding: the fixed icon is now stamped on ~200 library rows

`SongListItem.tsx:223` and `SongListView.tsx:1294` both fall back to the app's own `assets/icon.png` (`src={albumCover ?? appIcon}`) whenever a song has no album art — which is every one of the 243 unowned songs on the Songs screen right now. I confirmed this is not new plumbing (the old black icon was already doing the same job — cropped into `02-library-1225x768.png` from `2026-08-13-kb13-final`, it's the same dark snare-on-black square, just low-contrast enough against the beige row background that nobody would have flagged it as a placeholder at a glance). The side effect is new: now that the mark is a warm, legible, high-contrast badge, every one of the ~7+ visible rows (and, by the header count, up to 243 of them) carries the literal Drumroll brand mark as its thumbnail. Scrolling this list reads like every song was published by the app itself, not like a personal library with missing artwork. A neutral "no artwork" glyph — a plain note, a waveform without the ring/lugs, anything that doesn't double as the app's own logo — would fix this without touching the fallback logic's intent.

## Screen by screen, this round

- **Home** — the kit-bleed, one-line title, and text-safe zone-aware placement from `2026-08-13-room` all hold; every one of the eight strike zones (including tom1, previously the one bare ring) now carries a real color wash, confirmed by direct crop. One residual issue, not previously named: the title/kicker sits with effectively zero top margin, flush to the very top pixel row of the window, where `My Vibe` sits with generous space above and below it inside its own dark field. The four-line pile-up is gone; the "room to breathe" the acceptance notes ask for at the top edge specifically is not quite there yet.
- **Songs** — layout and copy unchanged from the already-passing kb13-final state. The new defect is the brand-mark-as-placeholder issue above, which this screen makes visible for the first time now that the icon isn't self-camouflaging.
- **Journey** — season rail, lock states, and the controls button all hold. The stray truncation-ellipsis dot after "Foundations" in the top-right season-stage plaque, first noticed in `2026-08-13-room`, is still there, confirmed again by nearest-neighbor crop at 1225×768 (absent at 1024×700 — the plaque box's `min(16rem, 34%)` width evidently binds tighter at the wider viewport). Two rounds old, still untouched, still low severity.
- **Practice** — transport line, mode/speed, and word-boundary truncation all hold at both widths. The staff itself is still a narrow ribbon of actual notation inside a much taller highlighted band — roughly three-quarters of the pink-washed region above/below the four visible beats is bare paper. This has been named and deliberately deprioritized twice already; naming it a third time, still not urgent relative to what's above.
- **Result** — the truthful headline/subtext/two-cell pattern from `2026-08-13-truth` holds. New in this pass: a roughly 250px dead gap between the collapsed "See the evidence" disclosure and the bottom evidence-summary box, present at both 1225×768 and 1024×700. This is structural, not an artifact of the captured zero-hit run — the receipt's content block (headline, cells, note, disclosure) doesn't grow with real data since the extra detail lives inside the still-collapsed disclosure, so a genuine scored run would leave the same gap. This is the same "empty space must be deliberate, not leftover" defect class already found and fixed once on Home; it has resurfaced in a different component.

## Ranked, most severe first, of everything still wrong

1. **The installed, notarized app (`/Applications/Drumroll.app`, build 1.2.13, and the packaged DMG in `release/kb13-final-20260813T062753Z`) still ships the old black/rejected icon, byte-identical to the pre-fix `.icns`, and this is true everywhere in that app including the in-app sidebar mark, not just the OS-drawn Dock/Finder icon.** The new icon exists only in `assets/` source and this design-qa proof folder — it has not been through a build/notarize/install cycle, so it has reached zero surfaces of the real app yet. The `2026-08-13-final` captures that appear to show the new sidebar mark were taken from a working-tree dev run (`capture-final.mjs` launches `out/main/index.js`, not the installed binary), not from the app the owner actually runs.
2. **The icon README's claim that the new icon is more beautiful than kb.5 at 1024px does not survive putting the two files side by side.** kb.5 is a richer, warmer, materially detailed rendered object; the winner is a flat, simplified vector badge. The winner is the right call for legibility (which was the actual complaint) and is a clear win over kb.5 at 16px and a clear win over the current icon at both sizes — but "better than both, full stop" overstates the full-size comparison.
3. **The new, now-legible icon is stamped as placeholder art on every one of ~200+ unowned library rows** (`SongListItem.tsx:223`, `SongListView.tsx:1294`, `albumCover ?? appIcon`), reading as self-branded merchandise rather than a personal library — invisible before because the old dark icon blended into the row background, exposed now that the fix worked.
4. **Result: a ~250px dead vertical gap between the evidence disclosure and the bottom evidence/CTA panel**, at both captured widths (`05-result-*.png`) — the same "leftover, not deliberate" empty-space defect already found and fixed once on Home, now present on Result, and structural rather than tied to the specific zero-hit capture.
5. **Journey: a stray, near-invisible truncation-ellipsis dot clips "Foundations" in the season-stage plaque at 1225×768** (`03-journey-1225x768.png`) — named two rounds ago, still unfixed, still low severity, still the same box-sizing mistake next to the rail-name fix that was supposed to retire this class of bug.
6. **Home: the title/kicker sit flush against the very top edge of the frame with no margin above them**, unlike `My Vibe`'s centered composition inside its own field (`01-home-*.png`) — the four-line pile-up that caused this finding twice before is gone, but the specific "room to breathe" ask isn't fully met at the top edge.
7. **Practice: the staff remains a narrow ribbon of actual notation inside a much taller highlighted band**, most of it bare paper above and below the four visible beats (`04-practice-*.png`) — long-standing, already deprioritized twice, carried forward unchanged, not urgent relative to items 1–6.

Nothing above is a notation-readability or truthfulness failure inside the running app itself — the receipt math, the library counts, the journey states all still agree with their own data, which is the bar the prior two passes fought hardest to clear and which is still holding. What's left is: one release-pipeline gap that means the actual deliverable hasn't reached the user yet, one overclaim in the icon's own supporting document, one newly-exposed side effect of a fix that worked, and three small, already-known polish items.
