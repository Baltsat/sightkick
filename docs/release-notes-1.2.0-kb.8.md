# Drumroll 1.2.0-kb.8

`kb.8` tightens the practice loop around one learner-facing rule: recover
without punishment, finish focused work, and move to the next useful action
with evidence intact. This is a build-in-flight note for source commit
`1ec1a4f`; it is not a publication receipt.

## Recovery that has a visible exit

- Resonance Runway puts a borderless cyan/violet escape path over the active
  remediation range. It moves through control, lock, and release from the
  actual clean-pass state, retained near-clean quality, and current-to-target
  tempo.
- The recovery HUD has a thin Coach caption and a reduced-motion fallback.
  Rendered proof covers retained quality, the first clean anchor, and release
  with a tempo rise.
- Coach ranges now split into at most four bars. Each short loop keeps its
  original review link, begins at 0.7×, clears through the existing quality
  rule, and reaches a finite terminal state.

## Enjoyment-first practice

- Practice starts with lives off and reports form or energy instead. Challenge
  lives remain an explicit opt-in; Perform keeps its separate contract.
- The score stays neutral while idle. At Ready, a pulsing Drumroll cue and a
  one-kick infographic make the next physical action unambiguous.
- Manual Practice launches now use the same learner-profile and song-difficulty
  speed recommendation as My Wave. Clean work can still release tempo upward.

## A more specific next action

- Persisted Coach remediation now outranks generic matching, even when a
  lesson’s authored tag differs from the finding’s tag. A practice wave still
  retains focus, application, and consolidation variety.
- Weekly skill targets are derived from the September 10 goal, prerequisites,
  and the measured eight-skill profile. The recommendation exposes a reason
  and degrades honestly when the goal or profile evidence is absent.
- Lessons 01.01–01.03 carry timing skill tags, so timing evidence can select
  their intended route rather than relying on a coincidental alias.

## Evidence and kit-reading refinements

- Kit color maturity has one evidence-based model: lane colors begin vivid and
  can fade toward black only as skill and scored evidence accumulate. Full
  color, faded, and near-black overrides remain available.
- Judge-evidence v3 retains the expected and actual tick/element for attributable
  wrong-pad events, plus the source timing window. Legacy records remain
  readable.
- New regressions cover direct Coach routing, manual adaptive speed, kit color
  maturity, four-bar remediation splitting and terminality, lives/form defaults,
  readiness cues, deadline pacing, wrong-pad attribution, every core route at
  1225×768 and 1024×700, and the forbidden “Support the project” copy.

## Evidence boundary

Physical Yamaha DTX402 cable-cycle behavior and a complete hands-free playing
session still require the connected kit. MIDI cannot establish posture, grip,
rebound, muscular tension, acoustic tone, or injury safety; learning efficacy
also needs longitudinal learner and teacher evidence. Protected Yandex audio
remains unavailable until lawful audio access and a reviewed chart exist.
