## Verification boundary

Automated curriculum validation proves stable IDs, prerequisite continuity,
playable generated files, lane content, tempo/mastery metadata, and the stated
three-tom coverage. It does not prove grip, rebound, posture, muscular tension,
acoustic tone, injury safety, long-term retention, or transfer to an acoustic
kit. Those remain human-teacher and longitudinal learner-study gates.
